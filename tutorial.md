# Tutorial Design

## Rendezvous

1. Select a vehicle
2. Right click another vehicle to set target
3. Maneuver to intercept (separation no greater than 25 km)
4. Time warp to intercept
5. Burn until low relative velocity wrt target
6. Burn towards target
7. Repeat (5) and (6) until rendezvous achieved

## Going to the Moon

1. Select a vehicle
2. Wait until proper phase angle
3. Orient towards prograde
4. Burn until orbit crosses Luna
5. Time warp

7. Profit
